Okay, thank you for confirming that — that’s a huge clue! If the printer shows as connected to the network on its screen, but still doesn’t appear in your computer’s printer list, we’re likely dealing with one of these common culprits:

––––––––––––––––––––

🚨 MOST LIKELY ISSUE: PRINTER NOT SHARED OR NOT DISCOVERABLE ON THE NETWORK

Even if the printer is connected to WiFi, your computer might not "see" it unless it’s properly shared or discovered.

✅ Fix 1: Check Printer Sharing (Windows)

1. Go to Settings > Devices > Printers & scanners.
2. Click "Add a printer or scanner".
3. Wait a few seconds — if the printer doesn’t show up, click "The printer that I want isn’t listed".
4. Choose "Add a printer using a TCP/IP address or hostname".
5. Enter the printer’s IP address (you can find this in the printer’s network settings menu — look for "Network Status" or "Print Network Configuration").
6. Select the correct driver (if prompted — search online for your printer model + "driver" to download it).
📌 This bypasses discovery and connects directly via IP — often the fastest fix!

––––––––––––––––––––

✅ Fix 2: Enable Network Discovery & File Sharing (Windows)

1. Open Control Panel > Network and Sharing Center.
2. Click "Change advanced sharing settings".
3. Under your current network profile (Private), make sure:
   • ✅ Turn on network discovery
   • ✅ Turn on file and printer sharing
   • ✅ Turn off password-protected sharing (for now — just to test)
4. Save changes.
🔁 Then go back to Add a printer — it should now show up.
